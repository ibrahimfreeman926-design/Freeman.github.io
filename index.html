🍽️ How Leefro works

Leefro is your own menu of moments. Post photos or short videos with a caption, like the posts you love, and follow people to build your feed.

Post — tap + or the Post tab, choose a photo or video, add a caption, and tap "Post it."

Messages — chat with people, send text or record a voice note with the mic button.

Profile — update your photo and nickname, see who follows you, and tune your settings below.
